What it is

Tirtha is a live 3D pilgrimage atlas of India. It covers 31 shrines across Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Sufi, Jain, and Christian traditions. A visitor can orbit the satellite Earth, walk the streets, walk inside a temple model, and open dated public photographs of the real shrine.

The live site is tirtha-ten.vercel.app. The source is the MIT-licensed repository PratapVijaySingh/tirtha. The app is Vite and Three.js, with a globe viewer, and it is hosted on Vercel.
