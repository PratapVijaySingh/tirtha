Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. Tirtha is a 3D pilgrimage atlas of India at tirtha-ten.vercel.app.

What this site does

Tirtha shows maps, visitor facts and 3D shrine models in your browser. It does not require an account. We do not run our own user database.

Information in your browser

  • An optional Google Maps key you paste in Settings stays in this browser’s local storage only.
  • If you allow location, it is used on your device to estimate distance to a shrine. It is not sent to our servers.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

After Google approves the site, Tirtha may show ads using Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and personalise ads, measure them, and stop fraud. This can include ads based on your visits to this site and other sites.

You can opt out of personalised Google ads at Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses data at Google advertising technologies.

Ads are placed in the shrine information panel, not on the 3D globe or temple canvas, to avoid accidental clicks.

Other third parties

Satellite imagery and place labels come from Esri. Weather comes from Open-Meteo. Real shrine photographs and encyclopedia images come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. Cloud texture by Solar System Scope, licensed CC BY 4.0. If you turn on photorealistic 3D, Google Map Tiles may process map views with the key you supply.

Children

Tirtha is a general-audience atlas of pilgrimage places. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
